A message is not its own proof.

SEAL looks for independently checkable claims, not visual polish or official-sounding language.

1

Identify the action

Separate what the message asks you to do from the names, numbers, and other details around it.

2

Check official evidence

Compare supported claims with independent official sources. See the source and the reason behind each finding.

3

Find a safer route

When available, use independently sourced court contact details instead of numbers or links in the message.

What a check can—and cannot—tell you.

SEAL reports MATCH, MISMATCH, or COULD NOT VERIFY for specific claims against available sources. When evidence is incomplete or official sources disagree, the responsible result is to say so.

A source match does not authenticate a message, prove who sent it, or establish that a summons is legally valid. SEAL is independent and is not affiliated with any court. If a message demands payment or sensitive information, contact the court using a number you find independently.
