Limits of a result

SEAL returns MATCH, MISMATCH, or COULD NOT VERIFY for individual claims against available evidence. An inconclusive result is not proof that a message is genuine or fraudulent.

A match does not authenticate the sender, prove a document is genuine, or establish legal validity. SEAL is independent and is not affiliated with any court. For urgent payment requests or demands for sensitive information, contact the court using independently sourced contact details.
