# Submission notes

**Pitch:** Someone sends you a court notice. SEAL checks with the court before you obey it.

**Product law:** The notice doesn’t get to verify itself.

Use `/demo` to record. The notice is fictional. Two institutional facts match, private fields abstain, and the specific SMS payment and official-contact claims receive sourced contradictions. Every red result links to official source text. Snapshot evidence is labeled; live source checking is independently available.

Related work and prior art: Riverside Superior Court’s own [jury scam warning](https://www.riverside.courts.ca.gov/news/riverside-superior-court-warns-residents-jury-duty-scam), its [Jury Services guidance](https://www.riverside.courts.ca.gov/divisions/jury-services), and established legal citation verification products. SEAL focuses on claim-level verification of an incoming notice with direct source linkage, not legal validity or document forgery detection.
